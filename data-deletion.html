Data Deletion

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Skyloons has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side storage. There is nothing on our end to delete, because everything is already only on your device.

Delete your gameplay data

Unlocked levels, best scores, star ratings, coins, the themes and cosmetic items you own, your missions and login streak, and your settings are stored only in local storage on your device. To delete all of it:

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Clear the app's storage from your device's Settings → Apps → Skyloons → Storage.

Either action permanently removes this data immediately. Since nothing is copied anywhere else, there is no waiting period and no account to close.

Delete your advertising identifier

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) to serve and measure ads. This identifier is controlled by your device, not by us, and you can reset or opt out of it at any time, independently of the app:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

See the full Privacy Policy for more detail on what Google AdMob collects.
