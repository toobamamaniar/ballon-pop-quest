Frequently Asked Questions

How Skyloons works, in plain words.

Getting started

Is Skyloons free?

Yes. It is free to play and supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases: you earn coins by playing and spend them on cosmetic items.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Levels, Arcade, Endless and the Daily Challenge all work offline. Ads need a connection, so without one they simply do not load and the game carries on as normal.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login and no profile. Your progress is saved on your device only.

Is Skyloons the same game as Balloon Pop Quest?

Yes. Skyloons is the new name. Your progress carries over when the app updates.

Gameplay

How do scoring and combos work?

Every balloon is worth points, and popping them back to back builds a combo multiplier: 5 in a row doubles your score, 10 in a row triples it, and 20 or more multiplies it by 5. Missing a normal balloon or popping a bomb breaks or reduces your combo, depending on the level.

What do the different balloons do?

Normal: the standard balloon, worth 1 point.

Golden: rarer and a bit faster, worth 5 points.

Rainbow: doubles your score for a few seconds and gives bonus coins.

Freeze: slows every balloon on screen for a few seconds.

Heart: gives back a lost life.

Armored: takes two taps to pop.

Clock: adds 3 seconds on levels with a timer.

Chain: a row of linked balloons. Pop one and the rest go off in turn.

Splitter: a big balloon that breaks into smaller ones when you pop it.

Bomb and decoy: leave them alone. Popping either one is a mistake and costs a life.

Boss: a giant balloon that waits at the end of every ten levels. Keep tapping to bring its health bar down.

What happens if a balloon reaches the top?

If a normal, chain or splitter balloon escapes, you lose a life. Missing a bonus balloon (golden, rainbow, freeze, heart, armored or clock) costs nothing, it is just a missed chance. Bombs and decoys floating away is a good thing.

Modes

What are Levels, Arcade, Endless and Daily Challenge?

Levels are 1,000 stages across 20 lands, each with its own goal, played in order. Levels 1 to 100 are the original set. From level 101, blocks of levels get a twist such as Gold Rush or Minefield.

Arcade has no lives, no time limit and no coins. You just pop, and leave when you want to.

Endless has no goal: survive as long as you can while the balloons get faster. After you lose you can watch an optional ad to continue, once per run.

Daily Challenge gives everyone the same board each day, which resets at midnight UTC. You get one run and a personal best.

Can I replay a level?

Yes. Each level keeps your best score and your star rating (1 to 3 stars), so you can go back and beat it whenever you like.

Coins and themes

How do I earn coins?

By finishing levels and bonus goals, the Daily Challenge, the six daily missions (and the bonus chest for completing all of them), the 7-day login calendar and the daily gift. You can also watch an optional rewarded ad for extra coins. Coins are never sold for real money.

What can I spend coins on?

Cosmetic items in the Shop: themes, balloon skins, pop effects, trails and pop sound packs. None of them changes how the game plays, so there is no pay-to-win anywhere.

What are themes?

A theme changes the sky and the look of the whole app, from the home screen to the shop. Starry Night is free. The others are bought with coins. If you had already unlocked a sky by reaching its level in an earlier version, you keep it.

Why did my theme change to Starry Night?

Starry Night became the default theme in an update, so everyone's equipped sky was reset once. Themes you owned are still yours: open the Shop, then Themes, to equip them again.

Do I have to watch ads to progress?

No. Rewarded ads are optional: continuing an Endless run, doubling your level coins or your daily gift. You can always say no and keep playing.

Troubleshooting

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Progress is saved on your device only, so there is no account to restore it from. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or moving to a new phone starts you again from the beginning.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen. Sound effects, music and vibration can each be switched off separately.

How do I report a bug or leave feedback?

Open Settings, then Contact Us, or email connectmaniar@gmail.com. If a level or a boss feels unfair, tell us which one.
