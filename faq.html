Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about how Balloon Pop Quest works.

Getting started

Is Balloon Pop Quest free?

Yes. The App is free to play, supported by optional advertising. There are no in-app purchases in this version — coins and skins are earned entirely through play.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. The core game — Levels, Endless Mode, and the Daily Challenge — works fully offline. Ads simply don't load when you have no connection; the game keeps working normally either way.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login, no account, and no user profile. Your progress is saved locally on your device only.

Gameplay

How does scoring and combo work?

Every balloon you pop is worth points, and popping balloons back-to-back builds a combo multiplier: 5 in a row doubles your score, 10 in a row triples it, and 20 or more multiplies it by 5×. Missing a normal balloon or popping a bomb breaks or reduces your combo, depending on the level.

What do the different balloons do?

🎈 Normal — the standard balloon, worth 1 point.

⭐ Golden — rare and a little faster, worth 5 points.

🌈 Rainbow — doubles your score for a few seconds and grants bonus coins.

❄️ Freeze — slows every balloon on screen for a few seconds.

❤️ Heart — rare; restores one lost life.

💣 Bomb — do not pop it. Popping a bomb costs a life and breaks your combo.

What happens if a balloon reaches the top of the screen?

Letting a normal balloon escape costs you a life. Missing a special balloon (golden, rainbow, freeze, or heart) costs nothing — it's a missed bonus, not a penalty. Bombs are also safe to let float away.

Modes

What's the difference between Levels, Endless, and Daily Challenge?

Levels are 30 handcrafted stages with a clear objective each, unlocked in order. Endless Mode has no objective — you just survive as long as you can as difficulty rises over time, and you can watch a rewarded ad to continue once per run after losing. The Daily Challenge gives everyone the same objective and settings each day, resetting at midnight UTC, with its own personal-best tracker.

Can I replay a level I've already completed?

Yes. Every level tracks your best score and star rating (1 to 3 stars), so you can go back and try to improve it at any time.

Coins and skins

How do I earn coins?

By completing levels, setting high scores, finishing the Daily Challenge, and by optionally watching rewarded ads. Coins are never sold for real money.

What can I spend coins on?

Cosmetic balloon skins only, in the in-app Shop. Skins change how balloons look and nothing else — there is no pay-to-win mechanic anywhere in the game.

Do I have to watch ads to progress?

Never. Every rewarded ad in the game (continuing an Endless run, doubling level coins) is entirely optional. You can always decline and keep playing normally.

Troubleshooting

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Progress is saved locally on your device only — there's no account to back it up to. Uninstalling the App, clearing its storage, or switching to a new device will reset your progress, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

How do I turn off sound, music, or vibration?

Open Settings from the main menu — sound effects, music, and vibration can each be toggled independently.

How do I report a bug or leave feedback?

Open Settings → Contact Us, or email connectmaniar@gmail.com directly. We read every message.
