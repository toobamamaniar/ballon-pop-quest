Skyloons

Pop. Combo. Conquer.

Skyloons is a free balloon popping game for Android. Balloons float up, you tap them before they get away, and the longer your streak the more every pop is worth. It works offline and there is no account to make.

Get it on Google Play How it works

What you get

  • 1,000 levelsOne big map split into 20 lands, with a giant boss every ten levels.
  • Combos5 pops in a row doubles your score, 10 triples it, 20 gives you 5x.
  • Arcade modeNo lives, no time limit, no coins. Just popping.
  • Endless and DailySurvive as long as you can, or take the same daily board as everyone else.
  • Make it yoursSpend the coins you earn on themes, skins, pop effects, trails and sounds.
  • Fair playEverything in the shop is cosmetic. Nobody can pay for an advantage.
